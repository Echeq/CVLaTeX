# CV guides by market

Source-backed convention guides for the markets CVLaTeX targets. Each guide is
a **snapshot of hiring conventions**, not legal advice: requirements change by
employer, sector and job posting, so always read the offer before sending a CV.

## School logo by market

The school logo is **optional in every market** and is not part of the standard
CV structure. Leave it empty for the United States, the United Kingdom and
Canada, where ATS parsers and conservative hiring practices favour plain text.
It is also uncommon in Spain, Latin America and Russia. In mainland China a
university mark can appear in campus or state-owned applications, but the
institution name in the education section is still the important part. In the
app it is drawn as a small letterhead mark above the name, in the left or right
corner you choose, with a locked wide crop for a long logo; when in doubt,
leave it off.

## What each guide covers

- Length, paper size and section order.
- Photo and personal-data conventions.
- ATS and file-format advice.
- Guidance for students, recent graduates and career changers.
- The closest CVLaTeX settings (template, paper, typography).
- Sources with links and the review date.
