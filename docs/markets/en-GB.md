# United Kingdom — CV guide (en-GB)

**Market snapshot:** UK employers expect a **CV** of no more than two A4 pages,
with a short personal statement and reverse-chronological experience. Photos,
age, date of birth, marital status and nationality are left out. British
spelling and day-first dates are part of reading “local”.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-19.

## At a glance

| Item | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Document | CV (not “resume”) |
| Length | 2 A4 pages maximum; 1 page is fine early on |
| Paper | A4 |
| Photo | No (except acting or modelling roles) |
| Personal data | No age, date of birth, marital status or nationality |
| Order | Reverse chronological |
| File | PDF unless the posting asks for .docx |
| Language | English (UK spelling) |

## Sections and order

1. **Contact** — name, city/county, phone, professional email, LinkedIn or
   portfolio.
2. **Personal statement** — three or four lines on who you are, your sector
   and what you are aiming for.
3. **Experience** — reverse chronological; employer, job title, dates and
   evidence-based bullets.
4. **Education** — qualifications, institutions and dates; most recent first.
5. **Skills and achievements** — languages, IT tools and relevant
   certifications.
6. **Interests** — optional; only if they add something relevant.
7. **References** — not needed at application stage; employers assume they
   are available on request.

Students and recent graduates can put **Education** before experience, then
placements, projects, volunteering and part-time work. One well-targeted page
often beats two generic ones.

## Photo and personal data

Leave out the photo for almost every role; UK guidance treats one as
unnecessary unless the job depends on appearance (acting, modelling). Do not
include date of birth, age, marital status or nationality: the National Careers
Service advises against them because they invite bias. A city and county are
enough; do not publish a full home address on a CV uploaded to job boards.

## ATS and file format

- Use standard headings and a clean single-column layout.
- Include the job title and keywords from the advert and person
  specification.
- Avoid images, logos and graphics that an ATS cannot read.
- Check whether the employer wants PDF or .docx.
- Use 10–12 pt Arial, Calibri or Times New Roman, consistent throughout.
- Keep roughly 2.5 cm margins and clear section headings.

## Common mistakes

- US spelling or US date order.
- A photo or date of birth.
- Titling the document “Curriculum Vitae” or “Resume”.
- Listing referees' contact details instead of “references available on
  request”.
- A long, untailored CV with generic phrases.
- Sending three pages without senior-level or academic justification.

## CVLaTeX settings

- Template: **Classic**, **Harvard** or **Modern**.
- Paper: **A4**.
- Font size: **10–12 pt**; font style: template default or serif.
- CV language: **English** (write UK spellings in the content).
- Export: **PDF** unless the posting requests Word.
