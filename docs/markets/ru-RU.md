# Russia — résumé guide (ru-RU)

**Market snapshot:** Russian recruiters usually expect a **резюме** of one to
two A4 pages in reverse-chronological order. hh.ru is the reference platform
and its structure — desired position, contacts, about, experience, education,
skills — is widely copied. A photo is common but optional; age and gender
often appear in templates but are not required.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-19.

## Sections and order

1. **Desired position (желаемая должность)** — the role you are targeting,
   with salary expectations only if the posting asks.
2. **Contacts** — city, phone, email, Telegram/LinkedIn if relevant.
3. **About (о себе)** — two to four lines: seniority, two proof points and
   what you are looking for.
4. **Experience (опыт работы)** — reverse chronological; company, position,
   dates, responsibilities and quantified achievements.
5. **Education (образование)** — university, degree, dates; additional
   courses and certifications.
6. **Skills (навыки)** — tools, technologies, languages and level.
7. **Recommendations** — optional; only with the person's permission.

Students and recent graduates can put education first, then internships,
projects, competitions and volunteering. One focused page is enough at the
start; two pages are acceptable with substantial experience.

## Photo and personal data

hh.ru recommends a clear, neutral portrait photo and a professional
background; it is still optional, and a CV without a photo is better than one
with an unsuitable picture. Age, gender, marital status and citizenship
appear in many templates but are not mandatory. Russian labour law prohibits
discrimination, so omit anything you do not want to be judged on.

Do not include a passport number, full home address, health details or
references' contact details without permission. City and willingness to
relocate or travel are enough.

## Format and platform

- Keep a simple, text-based structure so hh.ru and recruiters can read it.
- Use standard headings and mirror the vacancy's keywords.
- Quantify achievements: “increased sales by 30%”, not “responsible for
  sales”.
- Save as PDF; name the file with your surname and target position.
- A one-page CV is common for students; two pages are normal for experienced
  candidates.
- hh.ru also lets you publish an online profile; keep it consistent with the
  PDF.

## Common mistakes

- A holiday or group photo instead of a neutral portrait.
- A long, generic “about” section with no evidence.
- Listing duties without results or numbers.
- Ignoring the vacancy's keywords and title.
- Including unnecessary personal data.
- Sending a file with an unreadable name such as `Резюме_итог_2.pdf`.

## CVLaTeX settings

- Template: **Classic**, **Harvard** or **Modern**.
- Paper: **A4**.
- Font size: **10–12 pt**; font style: template default or serif.
- CV language: **English, Spanish or Chinese** for the section labels;
  Russian CV labels are **not available yet** in the app. You can write the
  content in Russian, but the labels will follow the selected CV language.
- Photo: optional.
- Export: **PDF**.
